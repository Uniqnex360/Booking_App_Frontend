What Happens to Your Data

In accordance with Google Play Developer policies and the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, the breakdown of personal data deleted versus legally retained is outlined below:

✕ Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted
  • Account Identity: Full name, email address, phone number, and password hash.
  • Profile Information: Date of birth, gender, user biography, preferred language, and avatar photo URL.
  • Saved Addresses: All saved delivery/billing street addresses, postal pincodes, and saved GPS coordinates.
  • Active Sessions & Security: Active refresh tokens, JWT authentication sessions, IP address logs, OTP verification codes, and password reset records.
  • User Preferences & Activity: Saved bookmarks, movie reviews, ratings, search history, and wishlist items.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Device push identifiers and mobile device tokens.
⚠ Data Kept & Legal Reason
  • Payment & Tax Invoice Records: Razorpay order IDs, payment IDs, refund transaction IDs, invoice reference codes, and transaction amounts.
  • Legal & Statutory Reason: Kept for 7 years pursuant to statutory financial compliance under Section 128 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Section 36 of the Goods and Services Tax (GST) Act, 2017 of India.
  • Handling: These financial records are retained strictly for mandatory accounting audits, tax reconciliation, and fraud prevention. They are permanently decoupled and anonymized from your active user profile.
  • Note: If you have never made a paid booking, no payment or invoice records are kept.
✓ Verification & Completion Timeline
  • Within 7 business days: Our privacy team verifies that the deletion request originated from the account owner and confirms receipt via email.
  • Within 30 calendar days: Your account profile, authentication credentials, addresses, and associated personal records are permanently erased from our production databases.

In-App Self-Service Alternative

If you only wish to remove specific profile information or saved addresses, you do not need to delete your whole account. You can instantly delete any saved address or update your profile details in the app:
Account → Edit Profile → Addresses → Delete.
